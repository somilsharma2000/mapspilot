# Sales Kit — MapsPilot

## WhatsApp pitch message (send to shop owners)

Hi [Name]! I found your shop on Google Maps and noticed a couple of things
that are costing you customers — [missing photos / no reviews replied /
wrong hours / not showing in "near me" searches].

I help local businesses get found by more customers on Google. I'll set up
and optimize your Google Business Profile (photos, services, hours,
description) so people searching nearby actually find YOU instead of your
competitors.

Setup is ₹2,999 one-time, or ₹1,999 if you already have a profile that needs
fixing. No monthly lock-in — but if you want, for ₹999/month I'll keep it
alive: reply to every review, post updates, and send you a simple report of
how many calls and directions you got.

Can I send you a free 5-point check of your current profile? No obligation.

## The 5-point free check (hook)

1. Is the shop verified on Google Maps?
2. Are hours/phone/address correct?
3. Photos — how many, how recent?
4. Recent reviews — replied or ignored?
5. Category & description optimized for "near me" searches?

Deliver this as a screenshot + 3-line verdict on WhatsApp. It converts.

## Objection handling

- "Why pay, I can do it myself" → "You absolutely can. Most owners plan to
  for 2 years and never get to it. We do it in 48 hours, and the monthly
  replies never get skipped."
- "Too costly" → "One customer from Maps usually pays it back. If your
  profile brings even 5 extra customers a month, it's the cheapest
  advertising you'll ever buy."
- "How do I know it works" → "Free 5-point check first, and after 30 days
  I show you the calls/directions numbers from Google's own report."

## Target list (fill as you walk the market)

- Salons & barbers
- Clinics & dentists
- Gyms & trainers
- Cafes & restaurants
- Electricians / plumbers / AC repair
- Coaching centers & tutors
- Boutiques & mobile shops

Rule: visit or message 10 shops a day, free-check the interested ones.
Close 1-2. That's ₹3-6k/day in Phase 1.
